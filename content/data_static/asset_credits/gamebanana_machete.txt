Installation Instructions:
--------------------------------

Required:

First of all open the Required folder and Select/Copy All folders to your Cstrike folder
Located here: C:\Program Files\Steam\steamapps\USERLOGIN\counter-strike source\cstrike
If it ask to overwrite the Files/Folders press yes to All.

Texture Choose:

Now go back the the root folder and go in to the Texture Choose folder.
Here you can choose between 3 different textures "clean, rusty, bloody" Choose witch one you want and open the folder.

Than Select/Copy the material folders and also paste it in your Cstrike folder, same location as before.
Again if it ask to overwrite the Files/Folders press yes to All.

Animation Choose:

Again go back the the root folder and go in to the Animations Choose folder.
Here you can choose between Syncings or DMG's Animation, Choose witch one you want and open the folder.

Than Select/Copy All folders and also paste it in your Cstrike folder, same location as before.
Again if it ask to overwrite the Files/Folders press yes to All.

Now you are done, hope you like my version of the FC2 Machete.
